Indicative, not representative (why)

EPDs ≠ PCRs. ECO Platform reports ~33,009 EPDs across 22 operators — but those are governed by only ~600–750 PCRs (the rules PCRbase inventories). The global PCR universe is ~1,120–2,050 (mid ~1,586).

Current DB = 43 PCRs / 4 programs → 18.3% of universe.
But method & language diversity is fully proven: all 3 method families (ISO 14067, EN 15804, PEF) and non-English extraction (Norwegian) work end-to-end. The gap is volume & geography (no US/Asia yet), not capability.

How many PCRs exist globally? — statistical estimate (Monte Carlo, 100,000 trials)

Each of the 17 segments is modelled as a triangular(low, mode, high) distribution (mode = hard enumerated count where known, else band midpoint), then summed over 100,000 independent draws. Summing the segment lows/highs naively assumes every segment hits its extreme at once (perfect correlation); the independent sum is the statistically correct, tighter interval.
